From the frontlines

Real questions from founders on building, optimizing, and scaling B2B SaaS products

What background do you bring to my startup?

Over 20 years of experience shaping B2B, SaaS, and complex software products. I’ve spent 12 years leading product design from zero to one for five startups, while also helping established teams rethink, optimize, and elevate existing products to drive measurable growth. I leverage this experience to help you bypass common traps and accelerate your roadmap.

Why not just use AI design tools instead of hiring a designer?

AI tools are great for fast drafting, but relying on them early locks your product into generic patterns. Design is not about making things look pretty or rounding corners. It requires deep human empathy, business context, and strategic judgment to architect complex B2B workflows that AI simply cannot build.

When is the right time to bring in strategic product design?

Whether you are building a new product from scratch or optimizing an existing product, bringing in dedicated product strategy makes all the difference. I help early-stage startups set the right foundation from day one, and work with existing products to eliminate friction, boost retention, and scale effectively.

What is the difference between a strategic product designer and a traditional UX/UI designer?

A traditional UX/UI designer usually focuses on executing specific screens based on set specifications. A strategic product partner works directly with founders and CPOs to shape core vision, validate decisions, and align design with business growth, whether building a new product from scratch or transforming an existing one.

Full time, agency, or fractional product strategist: Which should I choose?

Full-time hires bring high overhead and long ramp-up times. Agencies move fast on visuals but rarely invest deeply in your core business logic. A fractional strategist gives you senior-level product direction and hands-on execution exactly when you need it, minus the full-time commitment.

How does product design help reduce user churn?

Churn usually stems from onboarding friction or unclear value. Strategic product design pinpoints friction points, simplifies complex B2B/SaaS workflows, and guides users to their “Aha!” moment as fast as possible.

What is discovery and strategy, and why do it before designing screens?

Discovery defines what to build and why before writing code or pushing pixels. It involves mapping user journeys, unpicking complexity, and aligning on business goals so every designed screen serves a clear purpose.

How do we collaborate if the founding team is non-technical or non-design?

We speak the language of business goals, logic, and user value, not design jargon. My job is to translate your vision into a clear, effortless user experience through transparent communication and fast iterations.

Can design fix every user acquisition or retention problem?

Design cannot fix a lack of product-market fit. However, a strong product design process quickly reveals where the gap is, whether in positioning, messaging, or usability, helping you pivot with confidence.
